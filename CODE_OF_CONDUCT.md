# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Everyone taking part in issues, pull requests and discussions is expected to follow it: be respectful, assume good
intent, and keep criticism about the work.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting
(Security tab › "Report a vulnerability"), marking it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially; the
maintainer may remove comments, close threads or block participants who do not follow the code.
